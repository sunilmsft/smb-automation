# 01 Lab Overview

## Purpose of the SMB strategy lab
The SMB strategy lab exists to identify, pressure-test, and prioritize practical AI-enabled offerings for small businesses before committing to full product build cycles.

The lab emphasizes:
- Real customer pain over novelty.
- Fast validation over long speculative build cycles.
- Packaging complete outcomes instead of selling isolated features.
- Local-first learning loops (Seattle-first outreach and testing model).

## Why this repository matters
This repository is both a thinking environment and an execution bridge.
- Thinking environment: opportunity analysis, idea comparison, trust/risk filtering, and decision logs.
- Execution bridge: links from strategy artifacts into real projects, repositories, demos, and operating context.

## Operating principles captured in this lab
- Keep build complexity manageable for early-stage validation.
- Prioritize low-trust-barrier offerings first.
- Prove ROI quickly with small pilots.
- Preserve rationale and historical context to avoid re-learning the same lessons.

## Hybrid classification rationale
This is a Hybrid Workspace / Innovation Lab because it combines:
- Structured strategic research and decisioning.
- Active cross-project portfolio orchestration.
- Publicly hosted, continuously updated knowledge artifacts.
