# 02 Idea Map

## The 11-idea framework
The strategy lab evaluates 11 SMB opportunities across fit, complexity, trust, and revenue potential.

### Starter set (Ideas 1-5)
1. Never Miss a Lead (home services lead capture and follow-up)
2. Restaurant Customer Engine (reviews, retention, promotions)
3. Booked Solid (appointment/reminder/no-show reduction)
4. Bakery and Retail Order Manager (order capture and reorder loops)
5. Neighborhood CRM (broader SMB customer relationship layer)

### Bigger plays (Ideas 6-11)
6. AI Communications Hub / AI Receptionist (voice, text, email handling)
7. Social Media Autopilot (content generation and posting workflows)
8. Permit and Compliance Autopilot (date tracking and reminders)
9. Invisible Bookkeeper (transaction categorization and bookkeeping support)
10. Cash Flow Crystal Ball (cash risk prediction and alerts)
11. Ghost Kitchen Lite (restaurant virtual-brand utilization model)

## Trust-barrier model
The trust-barrier model is a gating mechanism for sequencing what gets built first.

### Low trust barrier (build now)
- Ideas 1 through 8 generally require communication routing, messaging access, or operational metadata.
- These can be introduced via trial models with lower adoption friction.

### High trust barrier (phase 2 and beyond)
- Ideas 9 through 11 require deeper business trust, such as financial visibility or higher operational control.
- These are treated as later-stage opportunities after relationship credibility is established.

## Why the trust-barrier model is central
The trust-barrier model prevents premature investment in high-friction offerings and keeps the lab focused on realistic go-to-market sequencing.

## Practical effect on roadmap
- Near term: prioritize lead capture, communications automation, and lightweight operations support.
- Mid term: expand into deeper workflow automation once trust, distribution, and references are in place.
